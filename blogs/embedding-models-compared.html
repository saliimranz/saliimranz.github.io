Embedding Models Compared: What Actually Matters for RAG

January 30, 2026 6 min read By Ali Imran

I benchmarked 8 embedding models across 3 real-world datasets. The results surprised me — and changed how I build retrieval pipelines. Covers OpenAI, Cohere, BGE, E5, and others. Evaluates on retrieval precision, latency, cost, and the often-ignored dimension of domain transferability.

The benchmark setup

Three datasets covering different domains: technical documentation (~50K chunks), legal contracts (~20K chunks), and customer support transcripts (~80K chunks). Eight models: OpenAI text-embedding-3-small/large, Cohere embed-english-v3, BGE-large-en-v1.5, BGE-M3, E5-mistral-7b-instruct, jina-embeddings-v2-base-en, and a small open-source baseline (all-MiniLM-L6-v2).

Metrics: NDCG@10 for precision, p95 latency for speed, $/1M tokens for cost, and domain transfer (training on one dataset, evaluating on another).

Surprise 1: smaller models held their own

BGE-large-en-v1.5 came within 3% NDCG of the best commercial models on the technical docs dataset, at zero per-call cost when self-hosted. For RAG over corpora you control, the case for paid APIs is weaker than the marketing suggests.

Surprise 2: domain transfer is uneven

Models that dominated technical docs collapsed on legal contracts. The vocabulary distance is doing real damage. Models with broader pre-training (E5-mistral, BGE-M3) transferred better than narrowly-tuned ones — even when the narrow models scored higher on in-domain tests.

Surprise 3: latency varies more than expected

OpenAI's API was the fastest end-to-end despite the network round trip — their batching is excellent. Self-hosted BGE on a single GPU was faster on small batches but degraded on large ones unless you carefully manage VRAM. For latency-sensitive paths, benchmark on your traffic shape, not just throughput.

What I use now

For client work where they control the corpus and want low ongoing cost: BGE-large or BGE-M3, self-hosted, with a fallback to OpenAI for uncovered queries. For products where speed-to-launch matters: OpenAI text-embedding-3-large. The key insight: match the model choice to the deployment constraints, not the leaderboard.
