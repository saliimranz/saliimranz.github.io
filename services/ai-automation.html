FAQ

Common questions before we start.

What platforms do you use for AI automation?

Primarily n8n (open-source, self-hostable, most flexible), with Zapier and Make.com for clients who prefer hosted SaaS. The choice depends on your stack, budget, and where the automation needs to live.

How long does an automation build take?

Quick fixes ship in 1–2 days. A typical multi-step workflow build takes 3–7 days. Full production automation systems with monitoring and handover take 1–3 weeks.

Do you handle LLM integration inside automations?

Yes. Most builds today include OpenAI, Claude, or Gemini calls inside the workflow — for classification, extraction, content generation, or agent-style decision-making. Includes prompt versioning, cost tracking, and fallback handling.

Can you migrate from Zapier to n8n to cut costs?

Yes. Zapier-to-n8n migrations are a common engagement. Self-hosted n8n typically pays for itself within 2–3 months for teams running 10+ active automations.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes — monthly retainers cover monitoring, error response, and incremental improvements. Available after the initial build is delivered.

What if my automation needs more than $500 of work?

Larger builds are scoped as multi-engagement projects or custom retainers. The $500 ceiling reflects a single tightly-scoped engagement — complex multi-month projects are quoted separately. Email me with your scope and I'll give a fixed quote.
