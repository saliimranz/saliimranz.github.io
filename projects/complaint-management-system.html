Complaint Management System

Oct 2025 — Dec 2025 LiMove Logistics LLC
PythonLangChainFastAPIPostgreSQLRedis

Re-architected a full complaint lifecycle management system with RBAC, real-time workflow transparency, and AI-powered operational insights using the LangChain Core library. The system unified customer, admin, and internal team views into a single auditable pipeline.

Impact

Reduced complaint resolution time from days to hours while improving accountability, prioritization of critical issues, and stakeholder visibility across customers, admins, and internal teams. Auditable trail eliminated the previous “lost ticket” problem.

AI Leverage

LangChain-driven prioritization layer that classifies complaint severity, suggests next-best-action, and surfaces patterns across historical tickets. AI-generated executive summaries gave leadership visibility without requiring manual reporting.

Problems Faced

Aligning role-specific views (customer, admin, internal) onto a single state machine without leaking sensitive data was the central challenge. Solved by per-role projection layers on top of a unified ticket model. AI prioritization had to be calibrated against historical resolution outcomes to avoid over-escalating.
