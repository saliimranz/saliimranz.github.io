FAQ

Common questions before we start.

What exactly is AEO and how is it different from SEO?

SEO optimizes for Google's blue-link results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) optimizes for being cited inside AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. The signals overlap (clean schema, fast pages) but AEO emphasizes structured data, llms.txt, AI-crawler permissions, and content shaped for citation rather than ranking.

How long does an AEO audit take?

An AEO audit typically takes 3–5 business days. Deliverable is a written report covering schema gaps, AI crawler reachability, citability score, and a prioritized list of fixes ranked by impact-to-effort.

Can you guarantee my site will be cited by ChatGPT?

No — and anyone who guarantees it is lying. AEO improves the probability of citation by making your content machine-readable, structured, and crawler-accessible. Actual citation depends on the AI model's training data freshness, query specificity, and competitive landscape. What I guarantee is the technical foundation; the rest is content quality and time.

Do you handle agentic commerce readiness?

Yes. Agentic commerce readiness means an AI agent (acting on behalf of a user) can find your product, parse your offer, and complete a transaction or contact action. Implementation includes Product / Offer schema, ContactPoint with potentialAction, structured pricing, and machine-actionable URLs. Included in implementation engagements.

How do I know if AEO is working?

Three signals: (1) AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) actively hitting your site — visible in server logs after implementation. (2) Schema validation passing in Google Rich Results Test and Schema.org validator. (3) Over 4–8 weeks: improved citation when you query the AI engines about your domain. I include monitoring guidance with every implementation.

What if I don't have a CMS — can you still implement AEO?

Yes. AEO works on static HTML (like this portfolio), Next.js, WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Framer — whatever you're running. The implementation differs slightly per stack but the deliverables are the same.
